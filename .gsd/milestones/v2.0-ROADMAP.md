## Milestone 2 Key Accomplishments
1. **Touchscreen & Pen**: Resolved portrait-native digitizer coordinate misalignments across Wayland and X11 via affine transformation matrices; eliminated post-wake I2C controller lockups via unbind/bind sleep resume hook.
2. **Dock & Tablet Daemon**: Fully automated tablet/clamshell mode transitions, locking display and enabling trackpad when docked, and unlocking sensor-driven auto-rotation and virtual keyboard when undocked.
3. **Audio UCM2**: Shipped complete ALSA UCM2 profile package eliminating "Dummy Output" and restoring dual digital mics, headphone jack sensing, and hardware auto-mute.
4. **Battery & Thermals**: Reduced idle power draw from 3.5W down to 1.6W, boosting battery runtime to 9+ hours while keeping passively cooled 6W chassis under 60°C under load.
5. **Unified Installer**: Expanded `scripts/install_dkms.sh` to provide one-command deployment and rollback across all system modules.
