## Milestone 4 Key Accomplishments
1. **Flawless Early Boot**: Bootloader console and Plymouth render upright from the very first frame of power-on (`fbcon=rotate:1`).
2. **Clean ACPI Architecture**: Completely eliminated `AE_ALREADY_EXISTS` DSDT/SSDT root port collisions via early initrd CPIO table override.
3. **Rock-Solid Sensors & Lighting**: Orientation jitter eliminated through 15-degree deadband / 500ms debounce; smooth ambient light auto-dimming via exponential moving average.
4. **Natural Touchpad & Active Pen**: Enabled tap-to-click, natural scrolling, palm rejection, and calibrated active stylus pressure & barrel buttons.
5. **Storage & LTE Cellular**: Automated secondary MicroSD GPT expansion and out-of-the-box ModemManager FCC unlock for Intel XMM 7360 LTE modems.
6. **Data Protection**: Zero data loss on battery exhaustion through automated <5% emergency hibernation daemon.
