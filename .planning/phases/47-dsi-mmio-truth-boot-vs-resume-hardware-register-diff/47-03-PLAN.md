# Plan 47-03 — On-Device Capture Session: Deploy, One Boot/Resume Pair, Kit Registration

## Artifacts this phase produces

- `scripts/capture_dsi_dumps.sh` — per-arm capture helper (trigger echo, wait for the D330DSIREG dmesg line, cat the frozen buffer to `<arm>_dsi_regs.txt`, sha256, per-section line-count report)

## Objective

Produce the two checksummed hardware-register dumps the phase exists for: one of
the working fresh boot (GOP-initialized panel, image visible) and one of the
broken S3 resume (panel black, captured immediately inside the ≥18 s
inhibit-held window) (D-03, D-04). Purpose: 47-04's diff and mechanism claim are
only as good as these two captures; the binding protocol exists because prior
sessions lost captures to idle blanking and stale timing.

Output: deployed dump kernel, one capture pair, both dumps registered in the RE
kit manifest, manifest verify green.
