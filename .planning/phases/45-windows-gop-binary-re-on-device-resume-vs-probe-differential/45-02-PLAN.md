# Plan 45-02 — DMI-Gated Fix Attempt (≤5 CI Iterations) or ROOT-4 Honest Closure

## Goal

Turn the 45-01 differential into a DMI-gated (`82H0`) i915 change that makes
resume power the panel WITHOUT a full reload (ROOT-02), verified on-device with
register/sysfs evidence, with the backlight returning at the user's brightness
with no manual write (ROOT-03) — or, after ≤5 evidence-backed iterations, prove
no clean fix is achievable and close the milestone on the packaged recovery
(ROOT-04).

- Device tree state: `~/d330-kernel/linux-7.0.0` carries the Option 2 patch + prior variants (.dphy/.preclk/.predcs) — do not regress them
